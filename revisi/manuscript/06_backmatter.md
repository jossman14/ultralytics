## ETHICS STATEMENT

The data consists of publicly posted social media content collected for research
on illegal gambling promotion. No private accounts were accessed and no user
identifiers are published. Faces and other personal information visible in frames
are incidental to the gambling marks the study detects and are not the subject of
analysis. A detector of this kind supports human moderation decisions and is not
intended to act as an automatic enforcement mechanism, because the error rates
reported in Section 3 are not compatible with unreviewed action against accounts.
